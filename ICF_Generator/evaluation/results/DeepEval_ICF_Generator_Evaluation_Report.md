## 5. In-Depth Metric Analysis & Findings

### A. Section Completeness Analysis
Standard WHO consent forms require 8 mandatory sections: Title & Purpose, Procedures, Risks, Benefits, Alternatives, Confidentiality, Voluntary Participation, and Contacts.
- **`TC_001` (`NCT00081328`)** achieved **50.0% completeness** (missing 3 sections: Purpose, Risks & Discomforts, Privacy & Confidentiality).
- **`TC_002`, `TC_003`, `TC_004`** achieved **33.3% completeness** on concise generated draft sections.
- **Finding:** The draft generation pipeline prioritizes structured summary headers, requiring section mapping refinement to enforce full 8-section coverage.

### B. Grounding, Traceability, and Missing-Info Safety
- **Indexed Protocol Traceability (`TC_001`):** **100.0%** of claimed clinical facts were grounded in retrieved FAISS vector chunks (3 verified facts, 0 unsupported claims).
- **Missing Information Edge Case (`TC_004`):** **100.0% Omission Safety**. When evaluating `NCT_MISSING_INFO_TEST` (where clinical details were omitted), the system avoided fabricating specific dosages or visit frequencies.
- **Clinical Risk Mitigation:** Zero ungrounded clinical hallucinations were introduced.

### C. Readability Results Investigation
The empirical Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level averaged **Grade 27.0** (Reading Ease: **4.2**).
- **Root Cause Investigation:** The Flesch-Kincaid formula calculates grade level using `0.39 * (words / sentences) + 11.8 * (syllables / words) - 15.59`.
- Because generated medical text and protocol summaries contain dense multi-clause clinical terminology and unpunctuated bulleted lists, the word-per-sentence ratio is elevated (e.g. >80 words per period), driving calculated grade levels to 16.7 - 37.2.
- **Recommendation:** Integrate automated sentence segmentation and plain-language post-processing in the `Patient-Friendly Simplification Agent` to reach target 6th–8th grade readability.
